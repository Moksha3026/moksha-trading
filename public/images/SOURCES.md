# Image sources

## Moksha Trading production photos (`own-*.jpg`)

Shot in-house from our own export runs. No licence restrictions.

Every crop deliberately excludes third-party marks that appear on the
original garments — a STYLEXO chest logo, a VIRAT/18 name and number, a
KNIGHT RIDERS print, and the customer badges on the sublimated polo.
Full-garment shots are only used for garments carrying no branding at all.

**Do not replace these with wider crops without re-checking for logos.**
